Case study · Brazil Protecting the Lear’s macaw

The Lear’s macaw nests only in the sandstone cliffs of northeastern Bahia. A new wind farm was planned less than twenty kilometres away.

Before construction started, our team surveyed the nesting cliffs over three breeding seasons and shared every sighting with local partners. The results shaped where the turbines could safely go.

Three of the planned turbine positions were moved away from the main flight corridor, and monitoring continues during operation.

Results are reported to the state environmental agency every six months.
