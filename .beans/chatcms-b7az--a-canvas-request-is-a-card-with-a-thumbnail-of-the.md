In the chat stream, a request made on the canvas shows as a card with a small picture of what was selected, rather than as quoted text. The AI's changes keep their short summary with Show changes and Undo.

Source: `~/secondbrain/content/quiqr_chatcms__chat_interfaces.md`.

## Summary of Changes

A request made from a selection shows in the chat as a card: a snippet of the
page with the selection marked, the request, and "From the preview · page".
Choosing it marks the place in the preview. Each change has a summary with
Show changes and Undo.
