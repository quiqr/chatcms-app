## Purpose

A design made in Claude Design becomes a website managed in ChatCMS: its HTML is converted into a Hugo site with a Quiqr model, in one shot. A print design becomes a Quarto PDF project the same way.

## Open questions

- How the conversion is done; to be discussed before any proposal.
- The format of the manifest Claude Design sends with the package.
- Whether an implementer's template changes reach editors only after publishing, so their previews do not break (see chatcms-luvd).
